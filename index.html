Ghosted

Keep track. Follow up. Keep going.

Stop wondering who replied. Know who to nudge.

A job search means dozens of applications, and many never get an answer. It’s easy to lose track of who you contacted, when, and whether it’s a good time to follow up. Ghosted keeps all of it in one calm place.

  • Know when to follow up

    After a week of quiet, a card shows “Follow-up due”. Pick a ready-made message, copy it and send it from your own email.

  • Keep your momentum

    Earn points, build a streak that forgives quiet days, and aim for a weekly goal. Encouragement only, never pressure.

  • Private by design

    No account, no tracking, no server. Your applications live in this browser, and the app works offline.
